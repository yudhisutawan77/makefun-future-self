One funny text message from your future self, under 200 characters — no setup,
no context, just the message as it arrives.

> Future you here. I finally listened to all 400 voice notes you left me. You mumble. Also: the plant is fine. It was never the plant. It was you.

Entry: 144 characters, 144 bytes. `main.py` writes it to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `ENTRY_OUT` / `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when the harness sets one), with no
trailing newline.
